7. What could break it

These are operating and cash-flow failure mechanisms, not repetitions of the valuation hurdle.

  • Delivery and energization delays — Supply congestion or unavailable site power can postpone revenue and cash even when customers still intend to deploy. The factory pack already records phased execution and shipment timing shifts.
  • Customer funding reverses — Trade working capital supplied 678.8M USD56 in the latest half year, primarily through deferred revenue. Advances can be consumed as projects ship, while inventories and receivables absorb cash. A funded growth phase is not a steady-state cash margin.
  • Competitive content capture disappoints — Qualified cooling rivals and electrical suppliers can win portions of the same project. More IT power does not ensure Vertiv captures the full portfolio opportunity or preserves product margins.
  • Reinvestment and acquisition returns disappoint — Factories and acquired capabilities consume cash before their returns arrive. Integration, ramp inefficiency or remediation costs can weaken cash generation even if industry deployment grows.
