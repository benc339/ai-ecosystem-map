7. What could break it

These are operating and funding risks, not additional penalties for the reverse-DCF hurdle.

  • Customer and process allocation — Customer process changes, captive procurement or loss of qualification could reduce Resonac's addressable material sales even if HBM demand grows.
  • Factory ramp execution — Installed material capacity may not become qualified saleable output on schedule. Weak utilisation or inventory build could absorb cash despite customer interest.
  • Non-AI business mix — Weakness in other operations could offset packaging gains at the consolidated profit and cash level. Missing segment data prevents attribution.
  • Funding pressure — Debt service and required investment could constrain shareholder cash before packaging growth pays back. Debt maturities, financing terms and liquidity headroom are not bundled.
