Production capacity, delivery timing and contract recognition govern the volume term. Realized price and service mix determine how much that volume earns. Applying that share to the combined gas total would be a denominator error. Converting an existing reservation into an order does not create another equipment unit. GAP: delivered-product ASP, recognition schedule and incremental cash margin.

Identity: Equipment revenue = delivered gas GW x realized revenue per GW + recognized electrical units x realized revenue per unit + other equipment revenue. Consolidated revenue adds services and software and subtracts eliminations. Operating cash flow less capex = net income + noncash and other operating adjustments + signed working-capital movements - gross capital expenditure.

7. What could break it

Operating risks are separate from the reverse-DCF price hurdle. The factory pack establishes exposure, not quantified loss probabilities.

  • Reservations fail to become firm projects — Financing, permitting, fuel supply or project cancellation can prevent reserved gas slots from becoming delivered orders. A customer-count exposure label does not measure dollars at risk.
  • Capacity and delivery execution — Expansion targets may not become usable manufacturing capacity on schedule. Supplier, installation or acceptance delays can postpone revenue even when the customer remains committed.
  • Contract profitability — Input inflation, rework and warranty costs can erode equipment profit. Wind losses can offset Power and Electrification gains even if their delivery ramps succeed.
  • Advance-payment reversal — Slower bookings or fulfillment of previously funded orders can reduce working-capital inflows. Cash collected before delivery is not a recurring operating margin; adding net cash also requires recognizing the future costs tied to those advances.
