3. The money equation

Production and acceptance gate system revenue; orders alone do not create sales. The visible capacity plan supports a volume mechanism, not a complete cash forecast. Do not multiply a quarterly blended EUV price by a yearly Low-NA-only output count. GAP: matched product volumes, realized prices, service revenue and recognition timing. See dcf-gap:ASML:unit_asp_bridge.

Identity: Revenue = sum across product families of revenue-recognized system units times realized selling price, plus service and field-option revenue.

7. What could break it

These are operating failure mechanisms, not restatements of an expensive valuation. No numerical loss estimate is supported by the bundle.

  • Production and acceptance delays — Missing components, integration problems or unfinished customer fabs can postpone recognized deliveries even when demand is intact.
  • Customer spending reversals — A customer can defer investment or change its technology roadmap. Demand can weaken even if ASML can deliver the planned capacity.
  • Technology adoption economics — High-NA adoption can be slower than expected, while installation and support costs can absorb higher prices. Better productivity can also substitute for additional new tools.
  • Cash timing — Customer advances, inventory and acceptance timing can lift or depress operating cash flow independently of current operating profit. A historical cash margin may not be repeatable.
  • Export permissions — Tighter licensing restrictions could prevent systems or services from reaching customers even when demand and production capacity are available. No current exposure amount is inferred.

10. Takeaway

Pass. The capacity plan supports growth, but public guidance is not a differentiated edge. The price demands more than the supplied valuation cases support, yet missing profit allocation and cash normalization prevent a defensible short thesis. Resolve the unit-to-profit bridge and recurring cash margin before treating the valuation gap as an investment opportunity.
