3. The money equation

Count reserved availability or actual usage according to the contract, without counting the same capacity twice. The operational constraint is commissioned, accepted and billable compute, not announced campus power. Financing can bind before deployment. GAP: active fleet, paid utilisation, realised contract rates and the bridge from power to GPU-hours (plan/cards/coreweave.md; plan/ANCHORS.md). List prices cannot fill these gaps.

Identity: GPU-cloud revenue = billable GPU-capacity-hours x realised revenue per capacity-hour. Total revenue also requires any separately billed services.

4. Where the money goes

Cash capex consumed 10.3B USD6 (FY2025; dcf/out/CRWV.json, inputs.capex_last). Net cash was -30.0B USD11, with leases excluded from that definition (2026-06-30; dcf/out/CRWV.json, inputs.net_cash). Spend pays for itself only if customer collections cover operating costs, financing and equipment renewal. The bundle does not prove that return. India expansion remains an unvalued growth option. GAP: maintenance versus growth spending, noncash equipment finance, leases, research spending, investments and shareholder distributions.

5. What's priced in

At 55.60 USD/share12, the frozen reverse model solves for a constant cash margin of 3.1%13 (2026-10-23; dcf/onepager/CRWV.json, numbers). It applies that margin across the forecast, rather than funding an explicit transition out of the build phase. The historical range is -484.7%14 to -141.3%15, with the latest margin at -141.3%3 (history through FY2025; dcf/out/CRWV.json, inputs). The revenue path starts from consensus of 12.9B USD16 for FY2026 and 26.5B USD17 for FY2027 (2026-10-23 snapshot; dcf/out/CRWV.json, inputs.cons_rev_avg). Consensus is an estimate path, not the price paid. The stored revenue multiplier of 20.00x18 and discount-rate result of 3.1%19 are not valid price-clearing solutions while cash margins and net cash remain negative (2026-10-23; dcf/onepager/CRWV.json, numbers). Positive revenue scaling cannot fix that sign problem; discounting negative cash flows above terminal growth cannot fix it either. These outputs are diagnostics, not evidence of market expectations.

6. What it's worth (DCF)

Not valued. The build explicitly flags its negative cash margin as not meaningful for valuation (dcf/out/CRWV.json, flags). The retained scenario table is diagnostic, not a set of price targets. Revenue paths and historical cash margins drive its spread; faster growth can make the labelled bull result worse by compounding cash burn. The engine carries CFO minus cash capex forward, discounts it at a CAPM-style cost of equity of 8.8%20, and then adds net cash (2026-10-23; dcf/out/CRWV.json, scenarios.base and inputs). It ignores debt weighting despite the debt-funded business, while beta is missing. Terminal growth is assumed at 3.0%21 (2026-10-23 build; dcf/out/CRWV.json, inputs.g_terminal). A fixed fade extends rapid final-consensus growth. The reverse model inherits these limits. A sourced forward cash margin, financing schedule and consistent cash-flow discount method are required before valuation.

7. What could break it

These are operating and funding failure paths, not extra valuation discounts. Their mechanisms are distinct, but their outcomes can interact.

  • Refinancing and dilution — Equipment must be funded before contract collections repay it. Costlier or unavailable refinancing can interrupt deployment or transfer returns away from equity. The card reports 10.6B USD28 due through YE2027 (report dated 2026-10-18; plan/cards/coreweave.md; unchecked The Motley Fool reporting). GAP: verify the maturity schedule, collateral, covenants, lease payments and funding sources.
  • Delivery and acceptance — Power, cooling or equipment delays can defer billable service while committed costs continue. The bundled connection map supports the general bottleneck mechanism, not a measured CoreWeave delay.
  • Customer concentration — Microsoft supplied 67.0%29 of revenue (FY2025; plan/cards/coreweave.md). Changes in procurement, acceptance or renewal can therefore affect collections. Separately, supplier-backed residual demand should not be treated as proof of independent customer utilisation. Neither point establishes a current customer default.
  • Equipment renewal — Newer accelerators can lower the economic value of installed equipment. Refresh spending and weaker residual values can absorb cash even when current contracts perform. GAP: realised rental rates and replacement economics by equipment cohort.

Its negative-margin revenue multiplier of 20.00x18 is not a valid implied market belief (2026-10-23; dcf/onepager/CRWV.json, numbers). Neither output establishes what investors actually expect.

10. Takeaway

Not valued; pass. The bundle documents demand, but not sustainable cash returns after deployment, equipment renewal and financing. The reverse-margin result is a conditional model calculation, while its negative-margin revenue solve is invalid. Preserve the diagnostic outputs without treating them as targets. Rebuild the cash and funding bridge before choosing a direction.
