7. What could break it

Business risks below are distinct from the price hurdle. A valuation gap alone is not an operating risk or a timing signal.

  • Customer design loss — Losing a design or accepting weaker pricing can reduce revenue even if customer compute spending rises. Custom units and AWS share retention are explicitly unresolved in plan/ANCHORS.md.
  • Manufacturing bottleneck — The bundled card identifies TSMC as the sole advanced-node wafer supplier. Wafer, substrate or packaging delays can defer accepted shipments even when the design is retained. A third-party packaging forecast is not reserved capacity.
  • Cash conversion and dilution — Accepted sales may require inventory and receivables before cash arrives. CFO can include non-cash stock-compensation add-backs while shareholders bear dilution. The missing cash bridge prevents measuring these effects for Marvell.
