Delivered capacity of 850 MW9 and OCI revenue of 7.4B USD4 do not establish revenue per unit of installed capacity (Q1 FY2027; plan/cards/oracle.md). 7. What could break it

These are separate operating and financing failure mechanisms. They are not additional discounts layered onto the same reverse-margin hurdle. Exposures are identified by the bundled factory and financial records, but their magnitudes are not established.

  • Power and customer acceptance — A completed building or installed GPU does not ensure power availability or customer acceptance. Delayed energization can defer billing while lease and financing costs continue. The bundled power-subscription proposals are not proof of full campus readiness.
  • Customer collection — A contracted customer may delay payment, renegotiate or fail to fund future usage. Reported contract value and remaining performance obligations are not collected cash. This risk can occur even when capacity is delivered on time.
  • Unit economics — Capacity can operate and customers can pay while realized prices fail to cover power, replacement hardware and other costs. Falling GPU rental prices or shorter useful lives would weaken returns without requiring a construction delay or customer default.
  • Funding and dilution — Refinancing costs, leases and equity issuance can reduce shareholder returns even if site operations perform. Customer-supplied hardware and net cash capex labels may shift who pays or when, without eliminating contractual obligations.
