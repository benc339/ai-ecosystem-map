TSMC (TSM): how it makes money, why it'll make more, and what's priced in

As of 2026-10-23 18:40 ET. Capacity can grow. Cash conversion is the hurdle. At 418.51 USD/share1, the model needs a CFO-less-capex margin of 39.4%2 (valuation as of 2026-10-23; dcf/onepager/TSM.json and dcf/out/TSM.json). No differentiated edge is established; pass.

1. How TSMC makes money

TSMC earns manufacturing fees from wafers and advanced packaging, not the selling price of its customers' accelerators. FY2025 wafer revenue was 101.0B USD3 and Other Products revenue was 16.6B USD4 (dcf/out/TSM.json; FMP product categories, not separate profit disclosures). Other Products must not be relabelled CoWoS. Consolidated revenue of 117.6B USD5 generated operating income of 59.8B USD6 at an operating margin of 50.8%7. CFO less capex was 30.6B USD8, or 26.1%9 of revenue (all FY2025; dcf/out/TSM.json). GAP: product operating profits and the profit share attributable to AI.

3. The money equation

Model wafers and packaging separately. Wafer starts are not qualified output, and packaging capacity is not interchangeable across designs. The bundled ticker map supports a packaging delivery constraint but does not quantify its size. Yield, utilization and customer allocation remain open. A customer's accelerator sales or power commitment cannot be booked as TSMC revenue.

Identity: Revenue = sum of billable wafer equivalents by process times realized revenue per wafer + separately billed packaging and other services. Do not count bundled services twice. Modeled CFO less capex = revenue times the consolidated cash-flow margin.

4. Where the money goes

FY2025 capex was 39.6B USD12, or 33.7%17 of revenue (dcf/out/TSM.json). Management's FY2026 capital budget is 60.0B USD18 to 64.0B USD19 (guidance dated 2026-10-15; plan/cards/tsmc.md). That is 35.4%20 to 37.8%21 of the bundled FY2026 consensus revenue (guidance dated 2026-10-15; consensus fetched 2026-10-23; plan/cards/tsmc.md and dcf/out/TSM.json). This ratio is not a cash-flow forecast. Productive capacity can pay for itself through qualified output and utilization, but the bundle gives no project-level return test. No supported split separates self-funding investment from speculative capacity. GAP: capex allocation and payback, research spending, dividends, repurchases and strategic investments.

5. What's priced in

At 418.51 USD/share1, the reverse DCF needs a steady CFO-less-capex margin of 39.4%2 on the consensus revenue path (valuation as of 2026-10-23; dcf/onepager/TSM.json and dcf/out/TSM.json). This exceeds the last-FY margin of 26.1%9 and the modeled historical range of 13.3%22 to 30.1%23 through FY2025 (dcf/out/TSM.json). Holding the last-FY margin instead requires 1.63x24 times the modeled consensus revenue path, reaching 581.0B USD25 in FY2029 rather than the consensus 356.2B USD26 (valuation as of 2026-10-23; dcf/onepager/TSM.json and dcf/out/TSM.json). Holding the operating path fixed instead implies a discount rate of 7.6%27, versus the model's 10.2%28 (as of 2026-10-23; dcf/onepager/TSM.json and dcf/out/TSM.json). These are alternative model solutions, not simultaneous requirements or observed investor beliefs. Consensus is an estimate path; price is what is paid.

7. What could break it

These are different operating failure mechanisms, not repeated versions of the valuation hurdle. The bundle does not supply independent probabilities for them.

  • Manufacturing execution — Poor yield, delayed qualification or unavailable packaging inputs can postpone billable output even when customer demand remains firm. Installed capacity alone does not remove these gates.
  • Customer demand and mix — Order deferrals or design changes can leave new capacity underused or change the node and packaging mix. A shift between accelerator designers is not automatically lost foundry demand.
  • Cash conversion — Construction, tools and working capital can absorb cash before shipments generate receipts. Revenue and accounting profit can grow without a matching increase in CFO less capex.
  • Physical or policy disruption — An interruption to fabs, utilities or logistics, or a restriction on eligible customers, can prevent delivery despite sound technology and firm orders. No timing or loss estimate is assumed.

10. Takeaway

TSMC has a sourced capacity-expansion plan, but the bundle does not connect it to the cash conversion priced into the shares. Price 418.51 USD/share1 exceeds even the modeled bull value 355.98 USD/share31 (as of 2026-10-23; dcf/out/TSM.json). That comparison is a valuation hurdle, not a short thesis. Pass. Revisit when filed product profits and a reconciled capacity-to-cash bridge establish an edge. Packaging upside remains unquantified.
