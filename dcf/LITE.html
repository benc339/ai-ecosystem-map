3. The money equation

Count external sales only. A laser inside a Lumentum module is not also separate external chip revenue. Qualified substrate deliveries, usable wafer output, assembly and customer acceptance constrain shipments. The factory reference-system bill of materials cannot supply Lumentum's unit share or ASP. GAP: a matched product-and-period units-times-ASP bridge. Scarcity may improve chip mix while module ASP still falls.

Identity: Revenue = external EML units x EML ASP + external CW units x CW ASP + external module units x module ASP + OCS systems x system ASP + other external product revenue. CFO-minus-capex = cash from operations - cash capital expenditure.

4. Where the money goes

FY2026 capex was 451.3M USD20, or 15.0%35 of revenue. R&D expense was 356.5M USD36. Expansion serving allocated laser demand has a visible commercial rationale; project payback and newer in-rack returns remain unquantified. The subsequent AXT agreement reserves supply for 6 years37. It requires an initial deposit of 43.5M USD38 and a later deposit of 43.5M USD39, with later payment terms to be set in CY2028. These are contractual obligations, not proof of payment. Deposits become shipment credits; do not count them again as permanent expense or assume they are capex. The agreement postdates FY2026. The bundled DCF contains no factory-tagged capex guide or forward FCF-margin input. GAP: forward capex, deposit cash timing and a sustainable cash-margin bridge.

7. What could break it

These are operating, cash-timing and ownership risks. The valuation sensitivities above are not counted again as separate risks.

  • Substrate delivery and qualification — Reserved InP capacity does not ensure licensed, qualified deliveries. The factory substrate note identifies permit constraints. A delay in wafers, usable die or assembly prevents shipment even while end demand stays strong.
  • Customer concentration — FY2026 Customer A and Customer B represented 26.6%57 and 15.0%58 of revenue, together 41.6%59. Cancellation, redesign or bargaining power at these unnamed customers affects revenue independently of substrate availability.
  • Cash committed before revenue — Deposits, inventories and receivables can absorb cash before collections. The AXT agreement also creates shortfall obligations if purchases fall below commitments, subject to contractual exceptions. Shipment credits do not remove timing or demand risk.
  • Product mix and substitution — The pack reports cloud-module revenue growth despite lower ASP. Architecture changes can shift spending between EML modules and CW laser solutions. Winning more volume does not establish an unchanged profit pool.
  • Per-share capital structure — Preferred equity and convertible securities require a current as-converted reconciliation. A common-share denominator and headline net cash do not by themselves establish the value attributable to each common share.
