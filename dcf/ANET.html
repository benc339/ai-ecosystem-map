3. The money equation

Translate accelerator deployments into usable fabric capacity before estimating systems, Arista share, acceptance timing and net price. A topology with fewer switching stages can change system demand without reducing compute deployment. The pack does not quantify Arista's units, attach or prices (dcf/evidence/ANET.md; dcf/out/ANET.json, factory_bridge). This is an analytical gap, not proof of a physical supply ceiling. Never substitute NVIDIA shipments for Arista units. Do not add optics suppliers' sales or bundled software again to system revenue.

Identity: Recognized revenue = recognized system units x realized net system price + separately recognized software and support revenue. AI system units depend on deployed accelerators, fabric capacity per accelerator, usable system capacity, vendor share and recognition timing.

7. What could break it

These are distinct operating channels, not observed failures. The valuation hurdle is not counted again as a business risk.

  • Deployment and acceptance timing — Customer construction, qualification or acceptance delays can shift recognized shipments even when intended network capacity is unchanged.
  • Architecture and vendor substitution — Customers can deploy more compute while using fewer switches per deployment, competing Ethernet systems or an integrated alternative. Industry spending therefore need not translate into Arista share gains.
  • Cash conversion and dilution — Non-cancellable supplier commitments can become inventory and cash uses when demand changes. Slower deferred-revenue cash inflows can weaken conversion. Stock compensation supports reported CFO while dilution or offsetting repurchases impose an economic cost.
