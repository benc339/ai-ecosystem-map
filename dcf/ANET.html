7. What could break it

These are operating mechanisms to test, not observed failures. The valuation hurdle above is not counted again as another business risk.

  • Deployment timing — Customer construction, funding or qualification delays could shift shipments and acceptance even if long-term demand remains intact.
  • Vendor and architecture substitution — A customer could deploy more AI capacity yet choose competing Ethernet systems, a different interconnect or an integrated platform. Arista share could fall without any decline in total AI spending.
  • Cash conversion and dilution — Customer payment timing, inventory or stock compensation could make CFO less capex diverge from durable cash available per share. Stock compensation can support reported CFO while diluting shareholders unless offset at an economic cost.
