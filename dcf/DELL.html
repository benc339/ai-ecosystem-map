7. What could break it

These are business and cash-flow mechanisms, not additional valuation-hurdle penalties. Their effects can overlap and should not be summed as independent losses.

  • Shipment and acceptance delays — Component shortages or unready customer sites can delay revenue despite backlog. Factory testing does not supply site power or guarantee customer acceptance.
  • Retained-margin compression — Expensive components can expand reported sales faster than gross profit. Aggressive system pricing or standardized integration can limit Dell's retained contribution even when units ship.
  • Customer funding and collection risk — Financing originations and slower collections can consume cash after a sale. Inventory and supplier-payment timing can magnify the cash need. Adjusted FCF excludes financing effects that still need funding.
