3. The money equation

The binding analytical gap is the conversion from customer spending to TEL product-level sales and cash. The pack has no matched own-tool shipment and ASP series. Its AI data-center WFE threshold of 50.0%27 and industry sales multiplier of at least 5.00x28 from CY2023 to CY2027 are market forecasts, not TEL revenue multipliers. Application mix excludes Field Solutions and cannot be applied directly to consolidated operating profit. WFE floors do not establish an exact growth rate by division. The KYEC procurement lead cannot supply track or etch ASP without the original filing, product identity, order scope and delivery dates.

Identity: Equipment revenue = sum across products of revenue-recognized tool units x realized selling price. Total revenue = equipment revenue + parts, service, upgrades and other reconciled sales. CFO-minus-capex = operating cash flow - cash purchases of tangible and intangible assets. Customer capex, equipment revenue and device output are different measures; recognition timing and product mix connect them.

7. What could break it

These are operating risks, distinct from the share-basis and valuation-method problems above. They are mechanisms to monitor, not claims that the adverse outcomes have already occurred.

  • Customer project delays — Customers can defer fab installation or tool acceptance while retaining long-run AI plans. Orders then fail to become revenue and cash on the expected schedule.
  • Process qualification and competition — Alternative tools or slower customer qualification can reduce TEL content and pricing even when total fab spending expands. The pack does not establish product-level share gains.
  • Working-capital absorption — Inventory builds, slower collection or weaker customer prepayments can depress cash conversion despite accounting-profit growth. The FY2026 inventory release is not a recurring source of cash by assumption.
  • Customer access restrictions — Changes in export permissions or geopolitical access could prevent equipment sales or service to customers that still have funded investment plans. This is a regulatory exposure, not a claim about a specific new restriction.
