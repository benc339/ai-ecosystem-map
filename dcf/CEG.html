7. What could break it

These are separate operating and financing failure modes. The valuation hurdle is discussed above and is not counted again as an operating risk.

  • Plant availability and capital execution — Outages, longer plant work or cost overruns can reduce delivered energy while increasing cash spending. No plant-level schedule is bundled.
  • Contract pricing and counterparty risk — Capacity announcements may not become enforceable cash payments. Low contract spreads or customer nonperformance can leave volume growth without incremental cash. Contract terms are missing.
  • Delivery access — Grid connection and customer-site readiness can delay offtake even when generation is available. The supplied map supports a general timing concern, not a CEG-specific delay estimate.
  • Funding and cash timing — Refinancing, collateral or working-capital needs can absorb cash before shareholder distributions. The bundle does not quantify these exposures or reconcile debt to the operating forecast.
