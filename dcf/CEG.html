3. The money equation

PPA energy revenue is paid MWh times realized revenue per MWh. Fleet GW is not annual energy, contracted load is not delivered energy, and existing-fleet procurement is not new supply. The nuclear output item establishes physical generation, but supplies no contract ASP or AI allocation (YE2025; dcf/evidence/CEG.md). The binding analytical inputs are realized contract spreads, delivery schedules and required reinvestment. Consolidated revenue also includes purchased-power sales and other activities. The bundled DCF has no factory-tagged capex guide or forward cash-margin input; it still extrapolates historical cash margins (2026-10-23; dcf/out/CEG.json).

Identity: PPA energy revenue = contracted MW x period hours x delivered-output fraction x realized USD/MWh. Incremental project cash = incremental energy, capacity and attribute receipts - displaced sales - incremental operating costs - cash taxes - working-capital needs - required capex. Model CFO-minus-capex = operating cash flow - capital expenditure.

7. What could break it

These are operating and financing failure modes, not another statement of the valuation hurdle. They describe possible outcomes, not estimated losses.

  • Plant availability and capital execution — Outages, uprate work, permitting delays or cost overruns can reduce paid output while increasing investment. A signed contract does not remove construction risk.
  • Contract economics and counterparties — New contracts may replace existing sales rather than add their full revenue. Hedge unwind costs, price terms, credit exposure and customer nonperformance can offset gross demand.
  • Grid delivery and cost allocation — Generation can be available before a customer or connection is ready. Network charges, curtailment rules or required upgrades can reduce the net spread without changing plant capacity.
  • Liquidity and funding — Collateral, working-capital settlements, refinancing and project spending can absorb cash before shareholders are paid. A temporary cash recovery should not automatically become a permanent margin assumption.
