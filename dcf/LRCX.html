7. What could break it

These are operating and cash risks, not repetitions of the valuation gap.

  • Trade-access interruption — China represented 35.0%43 of September revenue. Additional restrictions could remove sales or service opportunities; the geographic percentage is not the share of sales subject to restrictions.
  • Customer investment reversal — Deferred fab expansions or technology transitions reduce equipment orders and acceptances, even if long-run computing demand grows.
  • Process or competitive displacement — A competitor's design win or a process needing fewer Lam steps can reduce content per wafer despite customer capacity growth.
  • Cash-timing reversal — September operating-asset and liability movements contributed 535.4M USD44 of cash. A reversal could weaken conversion without any loss of equipment demand.
