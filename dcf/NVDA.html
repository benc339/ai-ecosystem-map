7. What could break it

The operating mechanisms below are distinct from the valuation thresholds. They can interact, so their financial effects must not be added mechanically.

  • Qualification and delivery slippage — Delayed good memory, packaging yields or testing can postpone accepted system deliveries. Customer power and cooling readiness can delay deployment without an identical delay in revenue recognition. The pack does not quantify the timing bridge.
  • Mix and substitution — Customers can shift workloads toward alternatives or negotiate component-level purchases. More units need not preserve realized ASP, networking attach or cash margin. GAP: product-level prices and margins.
  • Capital support and customer credit — Equity funding and capacity support expose NVIDIA to customer financing as well as product demand. The documented CoreWeave investment of 2.0B USD33 is evidence of exposure, not proof of fictitious revenue or customer insolvency. Cash commitments outside the modeled free-cash-flow measure need a separate bridge.
