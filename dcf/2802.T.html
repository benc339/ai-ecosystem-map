3. The money equation

The missing link is matched film output and realized price, not a shortage headline. Package size, layer count, process losses and customer inventories affect film area consumed. Available film capacity and customer qualifications constrain shipments, but the pack does not prove that film is currently the binding constraint. Functional Materials includes non-ABF products. Substrate makers' revenue, prices and customer advances cannot be booked as Ajinomoto film revenue. The bundled DCF has no factory-tagged capex or forward-margin override; its valuation still uses consolidated historical cash margins.

Identity: ABF film revenue = sum across grades of saleable film area shipped x realized net price per area. Functional Materials revenue = ABF film revenue + other materials revenue. Consolidated CFO minus cash capex depends on all businesses, cash taxes, working capital and investment. GAP: same-period film area, realized price, ABF-only costs and annual cash capex.

7. What could break it

These are operating and cash-conversion risks. Model-definition risks are recorded separately in the valuation discussion and gaps.

  • Qualification and conversion delays — Installed substrate capacity may wait for yield, customer approval or other inputs. Film demand then arrives later than the announced downstream expansion.
  • Film economics differ from substrate scarcity — A substrate shortage can reward substrate makers without raising film prices. Management reports a company-stated film share above 95.0%64, but this does not prove current film utilization or bargaining gains.
  • Substitution and lower film intensity — Alternative dielectric qualifications or package redesign can reduce film share or content. Market-share language alone does not establish lifetime exclusivity.
  • Consolidated cash leakage — Food and healthcare costs, inventory builds and growth investment can absorb materials profit. The latest quarter's CFO 18.4B JPY65 did not cover cash capex 33.4B JPY66. A materials earnings beat need not become group distributable cash.
