3. The money equation

Postation relays ABF film share above 95.0%20. That supports an incumbent position, not a measured price premium. The binding variable cannot be identified from the bundle. Film use per package, usable capacity, realized film prices and customer consumption remain open. A finished substrate price is not Ajinomoto film revenue. Do not apply a film-volume share to group sales or profit.

Identity: Film revenue = sold film area x realized price per area. Film demand = substrate starts x package area x build-up layers x film-use factor x Ajinomoto share. Sold film area is bounded by qualified film capacity and customer consumption. Group FCF = group operating cash flow minus cash capex.

5. What's priced in

At 5,064.00 JPY/share1, the inherited reverse DCF requires a steady CFO-minus-capex margin of 14.3%2 on its consensus revenue path. The last annual margin is 7.5%3. The bundled historical range is 3.3%23 to 7.5%24, so the implied margin lies above that range. History is not a hard ceiling. Keeping the last margin instead requires a revenue path 1.90x25 times consensus. The final consensus-year revenue becomes 3.5T JPY26, rather than 1.9T JPY27. Keeping revenue and margin unchanged instead requires a cost of equity of 5.8%28, versus 8.4%29 in the model. These are alternative solutions, not simultaneous requirements. Price is what is paid; consensus is an estimate path. Materials profit guidance alone does not establish the required group cash conversion.

7. What could break it

These are operating and cash-flow failure modes. They are separate from the demanding price-implied assumptions above.

  • Substrate customer timing — Qualification delays or capacity bottlenecks at substrate makers can defer film orders even when demand for finished chips remains strong.
  • End-market and portfolio demand — Weaker server or PC orders can reduce film demand. Weak results outside materials can offset its profit contribution. Server and network exposure is not exclusively AI exposure.
  • Capital intensity — Expansion can consume cash before qualified output earns revenue. Startup costs and working capital can prevent higher business profit from becoming higher free cash flow.
  • Material substitution — Customer qualification of alternative dielectrics or substrate redesigns could reduce incumbent share or limit realized prices. The bundle does not establish the timing of such a change.

(a) What the price says the market believes. Conditional on the inherited model, the price is consistent with cash margin 14.3%2, a revenue path 1.90x25 times consensus, or cost of equity 5.8%28. These alternatives do not reveal a unique investor belief.

(b) Our variant. none established: materials profit exposure is much larger than its sales exposure, but that is public guidance. The bundle does not establish an unpriced ABF cash-flow advantage or a reliable short.
